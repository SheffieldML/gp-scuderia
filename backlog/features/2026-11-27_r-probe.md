### 2026-11-27
Created from the accepted CIP-0007. Started: R 4.5.2 is installed, none of the packages are.

Installed into the git-ignored `.rlib/` of the worktree: DiceKriging 1.6.1, jsonlite 2.0.0, yaml 2.3.12 (C only, no C++ needed).

kergp and GauPro first failed to build (they need compiled C++ packages: nloptr, RcppArmadillo, sass). Two toolchain faults on this machine, both worked around without changing the system:
- clang++ searches `CommandLineTools/usr/include/c++/v1`, which does not exist; the headers are under `SDKs/MacOSX.sdk/usr/include/c++/v1`. Fixed with `-isystem` in a user Makevars, and `CPLUS_INCLUDE_PATH` for packages (sass) whose own Makevars override the flags.
- R's Makeconf hardcodes `FLIBS` to Homebrew gcc 15 under `aarch64-apple-darwin23`. Homebrew gcc is installed, but the current version is 16.2.0 under `aarch64-apple-darwin25`, so linking failed (`library 'emutls_w' not found`). Fixed by overriding `FLIBS` in the Makevars.

The Makevars lives in the git-ignored-by-intent `.rlib/` of the worktree and is not committed.

Installed (R 4.5.2): DiceKriging 1.6.1, kergp 0.5.8, GauPro 0.2.17, jsonlite 2.0.0, yaml 2.3.12. Reproduce with `CPLUS_INCLUDE_PATH=<SDK>/usr/include/c++/v1 R_MAKEVARS_USER=.rlib/Makevars R_LIBS_USER=.rlib`.
